Thank you for participating in our survey!

In this study, you will evaluate how well a model generates a personalized caption for a new image by using information from the given context of prior visual dialogues.

Step 1. Review the Context (Shared Memory)

You will first be shown two image–dialogue pairs in the context. Each pair consists of:

  • An image
  • A dialogue between a User and a Model about objects in that image

These dialogues represent the User’s past experiences and memories. You should treat them as contextual knowledge that the model may later refer to.

Step 2. Review the New Image and Candidate Captions

Next, you will be shown a new query image that may include objects discussed in the past dialogues.

For this new image, the model has generated two candidate captions (A and B).

Your Task

Your task is to choose the caption that is more appropriate overall, based on the following two criteria:

  1. Context Awareness:
    How well does the caption correctly reference and use information from the past dialogues when describing objects in the new image?
    (e.g., names, personalities, prior experiences, relationships)

  2. Image Description Quality:
    How accurately and naturally does the caption describe the visual content of the new image itself?

Please consider both criteria together and select the caption that matches for contextual personalization and visual description.

There is no objectively correct answer — we are interested in your judgment.

Your feedback will help us assess our method!
